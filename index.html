About The Association

Based in the heart of Mandera County, Kenya, our association is dedicated to promoting the traditional values and techniques of International Taekwon-Do Federation (ITF). We believe in empowering individuals through martial arts, fostering physical fitness, mental resilience, and a strong sense of community.

Self-Defense

Learn practical and effective self-defense techniques rooted in centuries of martial arts tradition.

Community

Join a supportive network of practitioners dedicated to mutual growth and community development.

Discipline

Develop focus, respect, and perseverance through the structured training of ITF Taekwon-Do.

Our Showcase

Experience the power, precision, and beauty of ITF Taekwon-Do. Hover over the cards to explore our core disciplines.

Patterns (Tul)

Pre-arranged sequences of movements that simulate a fight against multiple imaginary opponents.

Sparring (Matsogi)

Controlled combat against an opponent using attacking and defending techniques learned in training.

Self-Defense

Practical techniques designed to defend oneself against sudden attacks when no weapons are available.

Breaking (Kyukpa)

Demonstrating the power, focus, and accuracy of techniques by breaking solid objects like boards or bricks.
